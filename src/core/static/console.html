Worth knowing

  • The camera only looks up. Tilt travels from the horizon upward — this car cannot look down.
  • Diagonals work without Ctrl too. Ctrl+Q quits Firefox on Linux, which would drop your control link mid-drive. Chromium is the safer browser for driving.
  • Switching tabs stops the car. Losing focus clears every held key and sends a stop.
  • One driver at a time. A second browser sees the video but cannot steer.
